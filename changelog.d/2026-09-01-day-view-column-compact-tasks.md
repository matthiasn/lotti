### Added
- **Your day is now always in view next to your tasks.** On desktop, a
  day-view column docks on the right edge of the Tasks tab, showing today's
  planned time next to the time actually recorded — the same view the Daily
  OS day surface uses. A calendar
  button at the top hides it to a slim rail or brings it back, its edge drags
  wider or narrower, and when it gets narrow the planned and recorded lanes
  become swipeable pages. The column's visibility and width survive app
  restarts.
- **The tasks list gained a compact mode.** A toggle below the search and
  filter row switches between the familiar full task cards and a terse,
  line-per-task list showing just the titles — easier to scan when the day
  view column is open. The chosen mode is remembered across restarts.
