### Added
- **Lock the desktop app down to a single category for demos.** Tapping the
  Lotti logo at the top of the desktop sidebar opens a hidden menu listing your
  categories. Pick one and the app shows only that category: the sidebar keeps
  just Tasks and Logbook, both filtered to the chosen category, and Settings,
  saved filters, the activity panel and every other tab disappear so nothing
  else can be revealed by accident. The same logo menu offers "Exit lockdown"
  to bring everything back, and restarting the app always exits it too.
