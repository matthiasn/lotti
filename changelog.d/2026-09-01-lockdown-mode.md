### Added
- **Lock the desktop app down to a single category for demos.** Tapping the
  Lotti logo at the top of the desktop sidebar opens a hidden menu listing your
  categories. Pick one and the app shows only that category: the sidebar keeps
  Tasks, Habits, Insights, Goals and Logbook, each filtered to the chosen
  category, while Settings, saved filters, the activity panel and every other
  tab disappear, and keyboard shortcuts and the command palette cannot reach
  them either. It is a demo aid rather than a privacy wall: project and label
  names in the task filter sheet and entries linked from another category can
  still show. The same logo
  menu offers "Exit lockdown" to bring everything back, and restarting the app
  always exits it too.
