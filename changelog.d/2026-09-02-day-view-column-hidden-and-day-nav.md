### Changed
- **The day-view column beside the tasks list is now opt-in, and it can step
  through days.** On desktop the column that shows a day's planned and recorded
  time next to your tasks used to open by default; it now starts tucked away as
  a slim rail, and comes up with the calendar button when you want it. Once
  open, it no longer shows today only: the same date controls as the planner —
  previous and next arrows, and a date you can tap to pick any day or hold to
  jump back to today — sit in its top row beside the lane toggle, in a compact
  form that fits the narrow column. Paging the column leaves the planner tab's
  own day untouched.
