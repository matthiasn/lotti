### Added
- **A project's tasks are grouped and sortable.** The task list on a project
  page now groups by the month each task was created, newest month first, and
  folds finished tasks into a collapsed "Done" group at the end so a long
  project leads with open work. Every group shows its task count and total
  estimate and collapses on tap. A new "Sort and group" control offers grouping
  by status, priority or due window, or no grouping, and ordering by
  actionability, creation, due date, estimate, priority, last update or title,
  plus a switch to keep done tasks in their groups. The choice is remembered
  per project.

### Fixed
- **The Project Tasks header no longer breaks on narrow screens or with large
  text.** The title truncates instead of overflowing, and on phones, narrow
  desktop panes and large text the header drops its total estimate (each group
  shows its own) and turns Add task into an icon.
