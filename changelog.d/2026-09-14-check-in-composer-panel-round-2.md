### Changed
- **The check-in composer's started chip uses your clock format.** It reads
  `2:05 PM` or `14:05` the way the time picker does, so the two never
  disagree; the Briefing card's last-failed-run time follows the same rule.
- **Check-in composer polish at large text and on desktop.** The caption
  under the field and the More row's caption shorten a segment at a time
  instead of wrapping on a separator or slicing a word, the failure cards
  are the app's own callout with their buttons on the trailing edge,
  Re-record wears its own glyph, typing under a failure card that still has
  a recording folds it into its one-line retry, and the recorder's hint and
  buttons sit at the field's own sizes.
- **The Briefing card carries the health band as a colour dot beside its
  word, keeps its age tag quiet, and gives every footer button a full touch
  target.**
