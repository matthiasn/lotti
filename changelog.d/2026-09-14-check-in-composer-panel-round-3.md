### Changed
- **Check-in composer: clearer failure cards and a steadier large-text
  footer.** A failure card's title now says the next step — "Try again, or
  type what you remember", "Allow the microphone to dictate" — instead of
  repeating the header, its recommended action is a quiet pill so the
  alert colour is the only colour on it, a refused microphone offers Type
  instead as a button, and when the buttons stack at large text sizes Save
  leads and Cancel sits beneath it. On desktop the text field is focused as
  soon as the composer opens. Recording and transcribing status words read
  in the quiet ink, with only the dot and spinner coloured.
- **Briefing card polish.** The health-band dot and every status glyph sit
  on the first line of text at any text size, a person without an agent
  gets a neutral badge instead of the AI sparkle, the proposals count is the
  same tag shape as every other count, and the card's footer breathes
  evenly.
