### Changed
- **Check-in composer: one colour and one retry per failure card.** Type
  instead is a quiet button on both failure cards, so the card's alert tone
  is its only colour and the pill is its only shaped action; the microphone
  card is titled "Allow microphone access" and its body ends "then tap
  Dictate", pointing at the retry that is actually there; the header's
  status words wear the alert ink on a failure, as the Briefing card's do,
  and a failure is announced once, by the card, rather than by the header
  as well; the caption under a landed transcript sheds its word count before
  its keyboard shortcut; on the phone Cancel sits on the content column;
  the recorder's controls sit on the trailing rail, and discarding a
  recording says what happens in this composer's own words: the audio is
  deleted, the check-in stays open.
- **Briefing card: a failed run says how long ago.** "Last run failed ·
  19 min ago" reads in the same grammar as "as of 3 h ago" instead of a
  clock time; the band dot is the design system's presence dot; the
  stacked large-text footer keeps the row height below its primary.
