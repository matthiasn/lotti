### Changed
- **Check-in composer: the field's own ladder holds under a failure card.**
  Under a refused microphone the field's Dictate is a quiet text action and
  its placeholder steps down a size, so the card's pill is the face's one
  shape; while transcribing, Type instead is caption-sized so the wait
  outranks the exit; the keyboard-shortcut hint only appears once there are
  words to save; the folded More row says what is set ("Good · 2 topics ·
  next time noted") instead of the field names; its sections share one gap;
  the empty field is two lines tall in the desktop dialog; the recorder's
  level meter is white rather than the accent that means pressable.
- **Screen readers hear less noise.** A landed transcript is announced once,
  not again on every keystroke; a held Save button says why it is held; the
  recorder's clock reads as "23 seconds"; the Briefing card announces state
  changes only, and its alert colour sits on the state word while the age
  after it reads as metadata.
- **Briefing card footer.** A gap separates the actions from the model row,
  so at large text sizes the primary button no longer touches it; the
  not-enrolled face's text reads in the prose ink and closes on "a
  briefing".
