### Fixed
- **Calls and meetings logged as check-ins now show up on the Day view.** A
  check-in is time spent with someone, but the Day timeline never asked for
  check-ins, so a tracked 1 h 15 call was missing however long it ran. A
  check-in with a length now sits on the recorded lane under the person's
  name, in their category's colour. One saved with *No duration* stays off
  it, like any instant note.
- **The check-in text field drew two frames while typing.** A second outline
  appeared inside the field's own border as soon as the cursor was in it.
  The field now shows one.

### Changed
- **Editing a saved check-in no longer offers Dictate.** Once a check-in is
  text, it is edited as text, and the button no longer raises the question of
  what a recording would do to the words already there. A new check-in still
  offers Dictate, and a recording is added below anything already typed.
- **"Ask about this task" is now a compact Chat button beside Read more** on
  the task's AI summary card, instead of a wide button on its own row below.
