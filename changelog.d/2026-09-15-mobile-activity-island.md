### Changed
- **The running-timer and recording indicators on the phone now float as one
  glass capsule above the navigation.** They used to be two square-bottomed
  tabs drawn to sit on the old full-width bar; over the new floating
  navigation they hung in mid-air above the chips, in colours the rest of the
  bar no longer uses. A timer shows as a red dot with its elapsed time, a
  recording as the live level orb with its elapsed time, and both at once
  share the capsule. Tapping the timer opens its entry and tapping the
  recording reopens it, exactly as before, and the capsule reads the same
  over the classic tab bar. Nothing shows while nothing runs.
