### Fixed

- **Day plan blocks now stay linked to their tasks.** A drafted block could be
  saved with no task behind it, so it tracked no time and moved no status.
- **A day plan is no longer lost to the way its energy bands are written.** A
  band given as a plain time of day is read as that time on the plan day, and
  the assistant is told where the working day ends rather than inferring it —
  each of which used to cost a wasted drafting round trip.
