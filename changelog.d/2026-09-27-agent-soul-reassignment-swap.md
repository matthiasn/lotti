### Fixed
- **Changing a template's soul on two devices at once no longer swaps the
  souls between them.** If you picked a different soul for the same template
  on the desktop and on the phone before they synced, each device ended up
  showing the soul chosen on the other one, until the next change. Both
  devices now show the same soul, the one picked last. The other choice is
  kept in the background rather than deleted, and removing or replacing the
  soul clears it too. The same applies to a template's improver.
