### Fixed
- **Daily OS Shutdown now saves what you do and shows your real day.** The
  end-of-day screen was a placeholder: its lists and numbers were made up, your
  reflection was thrown away and choosing "tomorrow" or "drop" for a task did
  nothing. It now lists where your recorded time went, the planned or due tasks
  still open — moving one to tomorrow or a picked date sets its due date, and
  dropping it rejects it — and focus, flow, context switches and the energy from
  your session ratings, with a dash for anything not measured. Your reflection,
  typed or spoken, is saved as a journal entry, and the note for tomorrow is
  written from your day and read by tomorrow's plan.
- **Deferring a task while reconciling a check-in works again.** It failed
  because no date was passed; it now moves the task to the next day.
