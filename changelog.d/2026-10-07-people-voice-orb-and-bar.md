### Changed
- **Dictating a check-in now shows the same voice orb as the rest of the
  app.** Recording a check-in — a new one, or more audio on one you already
  logged — used to show only a strip of level bars, unlike every other place
  you speak to Lotti. The recorder now puts the Daily OS voice orb under
  that strip, breathing with your voice, and the orb works the way it does
  in Capture: tap it to stop, or to resume after a pause.
- **The person page's Log check-in button is as wide as its label.** On a
  wide window it stretched across the whole bottom bar next to Dictate and
  Call. It now hugs its word and the three controls sit centred, the way
  the task and entry pages end.
