### Changed
- **Dictating a check-in now looks and works like recording anywhere else in
  Lotti.** Recording a check-in — a new one, or more audio on one you already
  logged — used to show only a strip of level bars and its own row of
  buttons, unlike every other place you speak to Lotti. The recorder now has
  the Daily OS voice orb under a short level strip, breathing with your
  voice, and the orb is the one Stop, as it is in Capture: tap it to stop, or
  to resume after a pause — a line under the orb says so. Discard and Pause
  sit beneath it, and while you record the sheet's Cancel/Save bar steps
  aside entirely, so there is one set of controls on screen at a time.
- **Leaving a check-in while recording now asks first.** Going back from a
  check-in's page with a take running used to leave the recording going
  with nothing on screen to stop it. The page now asks whether to discard
  the recording, and if the page is closed another way the small recording
  indicator comes back so you can stop it from there.
- **People's buttons and bars now match the rest of the app.** On a wide
  window the person page's Log check-in stretched across the whole bottom
  bar; it is now as wide as its word, with the app's usual record button and
  a round Call button beside it and the three controls centred, the way the
  task and entry pages end. The check-in page's own bar is laid out the same
  way, and its menu is gone: back and a pencil to edit sit in the top bar
  directly. On that page your note now comes before the "Next time" notes,
  and both cards share one edge.
- **Dates in People use the app's regular typeface.** Every date and time in
  People was set in a monospace font inside normal sentences ("last spoke
  Sat 1 Aug 12:44"), which made the section look imported from somewhere
  else. Dates now use the same font as the text around them, with
  table-style digits so a column of them still lines up. The check-in page
  shows the same date format as the list that opened it, and every time in
  People is on the 24-hour clock, so a check-in's chip and its note no
  longer disagree about when it happened.
- **Smaller People tidy-ups.** "Next time" notes on the person page are flat
  text instead of boxed tiles, matching the check-in page; the People summary
  card's two halves line up and "people without reminders" sits beneath them;
  the check-in chips are the compact size the app uses elsewhere; the
  reminders sparkle on the People list no longer wears the tap colour; "See
  activity" on the briefing card is quiet beside the card's one real next
  step; a check-in's page is titled with the person's nickname and its Add
  comment button is a speech bubble rather than a second pencil; a check-in
  whose words are still being transcribed says so as a status line, not as
  if it were the note; the Category row on the add-person form leads with
  its colour and ends with the chevron that says it opens a picker; the
  composer's text box rests at three lines instead of five; and a person's
  nickname is shown plainly, without quotation marks.
