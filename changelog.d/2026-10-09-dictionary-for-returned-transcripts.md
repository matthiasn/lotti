### Changed

- **Your speech dictionary now reaches every voice input, not only recordings.**
  Dictating into a chat with an agent, capturing your day in Daily OS and the
  first task in onboarding now send your dictionary's terms to the speech
  recognition and fix near-misses of them in the text right away. A Daily OS
  capture heard by a speech-to-text engine such as Whisper is also corrected
  by your planner profile's audio post-processing model before you review it.
