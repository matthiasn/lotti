### Changed
- **A check-in's page now looks like a task's.** It opens under the
  person's category and name, as a task opens under its category and
  project, with the same large title and the same square tags for when,
  how, how long and how it felt — each still a tap away from changing, and
  the feeling now shown with a face in its colour. A length or feeling not
  yet set is offered as a soft round pill, the topics share one tag, and
  *Edit check-in* now sits at the end of that row, where a task keeps
  *Details*, instead of in the far corner. The words the check-in was logged
  with sit on the page under a faint rule, where a task keeps its notes,
  instead of in a box of their own. In the check-in form, the feeling
  choices are now the same size as the chips above them.
