### Changed
- **The check-in form is now laid out like the app's other forms.** Under
  the note, How, When, How long and How it felt are each a row with its
  icon, its value and an arrow, opening the same picker as on the check-in
  page — instead of a row of chips and a feeling picker hidden under
  *More*. Topics and the notes for next time fold out of one more row that
  says what you've filled in; editing a check-in shows them straight away.
  While you're recording, the recorder takes the whole sheet.
