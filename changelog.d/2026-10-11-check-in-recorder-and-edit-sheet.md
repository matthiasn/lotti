### Changed
- **Recording a check-in now uses the same recorder as a task.** Dictating
  into a check-in — in the form or on its page — shows the recording look
  you chose (the orb or the VU meter), the same running clock, and the same
  ✕, pause and red Stop buttons as the recorder a task's microphone opens,
  instead of a recorder of its own.
- **The check-in form no longer punches dark boxes into the sheet.** The
  note and the topics and next-time fields are now outlined on the sheet
  itself, like the fields in the app's other forms, in light and dark mode.
  Editing a saved check-in shows every section at once instead of behind a
  *More* row that was already open.
