### Changed
- **The recorder has a calmer, studio-style layout.** Recording on a task or
  a check-in now says *Recording* or *Paused* at the top, shows the running
  time under the orb or VU meter, and draws the moving level bars from
  onboarding as a thin line across the sheet — teal under the orb, neutral
  under the meter — so you can see your speech over the last few seconds.
  Stop is one big red button between quiet Discard and Pause buttons. The
  level line holds still while a recording is paused.

### Fixed
- **The voice orb no longer draws dark streaks in light mode.** Its brightest
  moments now glow in a light mint instead of turning near-black, and the grey
  haze around the ring is gone — on the recorder, in the Daily OS capture and in
  onboarding. On the recorder it now sits on a soft pool of light. Dark mode
  looks exactly as before.
