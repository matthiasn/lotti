### Changed
- **Every recorder now shows the level bars under the orb or the VU meter.**
  Recording on a task or a check-in pairs the look you chose with the moving
  level bars from onboarding — teal under the orb, neutral under the meter —
  so you can see your speech over the last few seconds, not just how loud it
  is right now. The bars hold still while a recording is paused, and the
  running time is a little smaller and evenly spaced under them.

### Fixed
- **The voice orb no longer draws dark streaks in light mode.** Its brightest
  moments now glow in a light mint instead of turning near-black, and the grey
  haze around the ring is gone — on the recorder, in the Daily OS capture and in
  onboarding. Dark mode looks exactly as before.
