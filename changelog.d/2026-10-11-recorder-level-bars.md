### Changed
- **Every recorder now shows the level bars under the orb or the VU meter.**
  Recording on a task or a check-in pairs the look you chose with the moving
  level bars from onboarding — teal under the orb, neutral under the meter —
  so you can see your speech over the last few seconds, not just how loud it
  is right now. The bars hold still while a recording is paused.
