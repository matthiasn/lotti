### Fixed
- **A reworded reminder keeps its new words on every device**, even when
  another device's clock runs ahead.
- **Restoring a backup no longer confuses sync.** Changes made after the
  backup are not mistaken for missing ones, and new changes never reuse an
  old change's number.
- **A missing photo or recording is not requested again on every launch**
  once no device can supply it.
