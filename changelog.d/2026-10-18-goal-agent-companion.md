### Added
- **The companion on your goal's AI summary.** With **Companion** on, a goal's
  page shows her on its AI summary card the way task cards do: standing
  beside the summary on a wide screen, her portrait in the card's header on a
  phone — tap it to see her in full. She keeps the goal's pose from the Goals
  list, and with read-aloud on, a new speaker button has her read the summary
  to you.
