### Added
- **Make the companion your own.** With **Companion** on, Settings ›
  Preferences › Companion lets you choose how she looks: one of six skin
  tones, her hair as a bun, short curls, an afro, locs or a bob, its colour —
  silver, white, grey, black, brown, auburn, or pink, lavender and blue if
  she fancies — and an outfit she always wears, or "Surprise me" to let her
  pick one each time you open the app. Every choice combines with every
  other, and she changes as you choose, in a preview at the top of the page.
  Your choice follows you to your other devices.
