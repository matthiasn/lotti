### Changed
- **The companion's face follows what she reads.** With **Companion** on,
  she no longer reads a whole summary with one expression. Through praise
  she brightens and gives you a thumbs up or a cheer; where the summary turns
  to something overdue, blocked or behind, her face changes with it — a
  furrowed brow, a hand to her cheek — and back again once the words move
  on. She reads the tone from the words themselves, on your device: English
  and German words, and the ✅ and ⚠️ marks of any report. She stays kind
  about it, never scolding.
