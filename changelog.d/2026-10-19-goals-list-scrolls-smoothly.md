### Fixed
- **The Goals list scrolls smoothly on phones.** Scrolling down froze for a
  moment while the habit heatmap and chart at the bottom were built, and
  scrolling back up reloaded every goal card — the day's briefing even
  vanished and reappeared. The page now loads everything once when you open
  it and keeps it while you scroll; a goal's companion only moves while you
  can see her. On narrow phones the streak label also wraps inside its pill
  instead of running past it.
