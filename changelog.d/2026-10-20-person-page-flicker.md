### Fixed
- **A person's page no longer flickers after a check-in.** Saving a check-in
  — especially a dictated one, whose recording and transcript land in steps
  — rebuilt the timeline below the new check-in and, on a phone, the agent's
  briefing card, once for every step: photos blinked, and a card or a
  transcript you had opened folded again. The page now updates in place, and
  what you opened stays open.
