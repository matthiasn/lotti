### Added
- **The companion reads with sass, and you choose how much.** Settings ›
  Preferences › Companion now has a sass setting: warm, cheeky (the default) or
  sassy. She shows each level as you pick it. Until now every read was warm,
  whatever the words said.
- **Her face keeps moving while she reads.** A question gets one brow up, a
  "but" the side-eye, a slip that comes up "again" the look over her glasses,
  and praise she exclaims a smirk or a wink. A face lands as she says its
  word, for a moment and in the middle of whatever her hands are doing. When
  she finishes she has the last word: a wink for good news, a raised brow or
  the look over her glasses for trouble, the side-eye for both; a pause or a
  stop before the end is not a finish, so she keeps it until she has read it
  all. "Shipped", "merged", "released", "resolved" and "fixed" now count as
  good news.
