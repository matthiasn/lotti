### Added
- **Tell the companion something, and one tap files it.** Say what happened
  on a task and she proposes to file your words, verbatim, as an entry
  under that task; nothing is written until you tap. A filed entry can be
  taken back out. Anything she cannot place waits in a new inbox in her
  chat, counted in the header, with chips to make it a task, start a goal
  or project, or save it as a note. Goal and person statements go straight
  to their agents. Part of the experimental *Companion chat* in Advanced
  settings.
