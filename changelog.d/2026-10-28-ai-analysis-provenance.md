### Added
- **You can see who analysed an image or recording, and go there.** The
  details behind an analysis, transcript or summary now list the agent, the
  inference profile and the skill that produced it, plus the task it belongs
  to. Tap the agent, profile or task to open it.

### Changed
- **The AI pill on an analysis or transcript always appears.** It used to
  disappear whenever the provider reported no cost, which covered local models
  and Melious speech-to-text, so nothing showed that AI had written the text.
  The pill now names the model, and adds the cost only when it is known.
  Analyses from before the app kept this record show their model and say that
  nothing more was recorded.
