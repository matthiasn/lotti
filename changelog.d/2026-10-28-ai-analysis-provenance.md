### Added
- **You can see who analysed an image or recording, and go there.** The
  details behind an analysis, transcript or summary now list the agent, the
  inference profile and the skill that produced it, plus the task it belongs
  to. Tap the agent, profile or task to open it.

### Changed
- **The AI pill on an analysis or transcript no longer disappears when no cost
  was reported.** It used to vanish for local models and Melious
  speech-to-text, so nothing showed that AI had written the text. Wherever the
  app recorded who did the work, the pill now shows: on an analysis or summary
  it names the model, on a recording it names the transcription, and either
  adds the cost only when it is known. Analyses and generated prompts from
  before the app kept this record show their model and say that nothing more
  was recorded.
