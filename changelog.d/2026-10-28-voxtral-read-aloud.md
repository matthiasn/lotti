### Added

- **Summaries, goal reports and chat answers can be read aloud with a Mistral
  Voxtral voice.** Pick it in an inference profile's new *Read aloud* slot.
  Each language speaks with its own voice — choose one per language under
  Settings → Speech, or keep its default female voice. Profiles without a
  read-aloud model keep the on-device voice.

### Fixed

- **Mistral's Voxtral text-to-speech models are no longer offered as
  transcription models.**
