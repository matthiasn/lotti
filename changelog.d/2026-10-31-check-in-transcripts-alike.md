### Changed
- **Goal and People check-ins show a recording's words the same way.** While
  the words are on their way, every check-in recording shows a spinner with
  *Transcribing…*. When they are not coming, it shows a warning with *Not
  transcribed* or *Transcription failed* and one *Retry* button. Typed and
  spoken words share one text size, folding at three lines with *Show more*.
  This holds on a goal's check-ins and charter, in a person's check-in
  composer and on their timeline.

### Fixed
- **A person's old recordings no longer claim to be transcribing forever.** A
  voice note or spoken check-in that nothing transcribed now says *Not
  transcribed* ten minutes after it was recorded, as on goals, instead of
  *Transcribing…* indefinitely.
