### Changed
- **Goal and People check-ins look alike.**
  - **One timeline.** A goal's check-ins use the same timeline as a
    person's: the same row icons, one line saying when and what, and day
    headings in sentence case.
  - **Words shown one way.** While the words are on their way, every
    recording shows a spinner with *Transcribing…*. When they are not
    coming, it says *Not transcribed* or *Transcription failed* with one
    *Retry*. Typed and spoken words share one text size and fold at three
    lines with *Show more*.
  - **Light playback.** A recording in either timeline shows *Play audio*.
    Tapping it opens the waveform player in place while it plays.
- **Goals have a category.** You choose it on the goal's last step, as for a
  person. The goal's check-ins and recordings are filed in it.
- **Changing a person's or a goal's category moves everything with them.**
  This covers check-ins, recordings, notes, photos and a goal's charter.
  Tasks linked to them keep their own category.
- **New agents for people and goals start on the inference profile their
  category names**, as a task's agent does. You can change it in the agent's
  setup.

### Fixed
- **A person's old recordings no longer claim to be transcribing forever.**
  Ten minutes after it was recorded, a voice note or spoken check-in that
  nothing transcribed says *Not transcribed*, as on goals, and offers
  *Retry*.
- **"Copy category default" in a person's or a goal's agent setup now copies
  the category's profile.** It used to turn the agent's AI off.
- **Screen readers can reach the actions inside timeline rows**, such as play,
  retry and show more.
