### Fixed
- **The companion's lips keep pace with her voice.** Her mouth used to drift
  behind what she was saying, closing for a comma the voice had already left
  and then shaping the wrong words, by a third of a second within one short
  line and more over a long summary. Her mouth now waits wherever the voice
  actually pauses and starts each phrase the moment the voice does, with the
  on-device and the cloud voice alike. Words ending in a silent e, like
  "have" or "time", now end on the right mouth shape.
- **Read aloud no longer makes odd noises at emoji.** The cloud voice
  turned an emoji in the text into a stray, garbled sound; emoji are now left
  out of what either voice speaks. The text on screen keeps them.
