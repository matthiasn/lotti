### Changed
- **The companion's mouth follows the sound of her voice, not just its
  pauses.** Inside a long sentence her lips used to guess at each word's
  timing from its spelling and could fall a quarter to half a second behind.
  She now listens to the recording itself — where it hisses, hums, opens up
  or goes quiet — and lines each sound of the words up with it, so an "s"
  hisses when the voice does and a breath between words closes her lips. It
  runs entirely on the device, the same on every platform, with the cloud and
  the on-device voice alike.
