### Added
- **Many chats with the companion.** Start a new chat for a new subject and
  come back to an earlier one: the list groups them by day, each with what
  she last said, and lets you search, rename, archive or delete them. Each
  chat keeps its own context, and the inbox still gathers what waits on you
  from all of them. Your earlier messages are kept as one chat.

### Changed
- **Talking to the companion leads with her.** A chat opens on her, her
  answers read as plain prose, and one large button lets you talk: tap to
  speak, tap again to send what you said. Typing is a tap away and stays
  your choice for the session.
