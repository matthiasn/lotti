### Added
- **A goal can say its check-ins are the point.** A journal tracked as a habit
  ticked once a day used to have its check-ins boiled down to "they checked
  in", so its agent kept noting that you wrote and never what you wrote. The
  goal form now has a "My check-ins are the point" switch: turned on, the agent
  keeps the themes, decisions and questions of every check-in, answers them in
  its report, and refreshes that report minutes after a new one lands. Goals
  measured by check-in time start with it on; every other goal starts with it
  off.
