### Added
- **Add to a project from its page.** A bar at the foot of a project records
  a voice note, writes a note, adds an image, or opens Add for the rest —
  all linked to the project, in its timeline.

### Changed
- **The project's agent reads what you add.** Notes, voice notes and images
  linked to a project — including what an image shows — are in its agent's
  context, as a task's entries are in its agent's.
