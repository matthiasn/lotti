### Added
- **The task agent finds the task a note means, in any language.** When a
  note, a log entry or a transcript speaks of other work — "blocked by the
  relay repair", "follow-up to the census export", "same problem as last
  week" — the agent searches your tasks by meaning, on the device, reads the
  summary of a likely match, and proposes the link with the right kind of
  relationship. It searches only when a note calls for it, never looks at
  private tasks or outside the categories it works in, and links nothing
  until you accept.
- **See which task a proposed link means before you decide.** The task's
  title in a link proposal is now a link: it opens a preview with that
  task's summary and a button to open it. Link proposals also read in your
  language rather than in English.
