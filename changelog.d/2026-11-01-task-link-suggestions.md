### Added
- **A task now suggests links its notes already imply.** When a note, a log
  entry or a transcript speaks of other work — "blocked by the relay repair",
  "follow-up to the census export", "same problem as last week" — the task it
  most likely means is found by meaning, on the device, and offered in the
  task's proposed changes as a link of the matching kind: blocked by, follows
  up on, fixes, duplicates, supersedes or simply related. Nothing is linked
  until you accept it; dismissing one hides it on that device, and a task
  that is already linked is never suggested. Tap the suggested task's title
  to see what it is about — its summary, with a button to open it — before
  you decide.
- **The task agent finds the task a note means, in any language.** When a
  note speaks of other work, the agent searches your tasks by meaning, reads
  the summary of a likely match, and proposes the link with the right kind
  of relationship. It searches only when a note calls for it, and never
  looks at private tasks or outside the categories it works in. It needs search by meaning
  switched on and its model downloaded, and understands all of the app's
  languages.
