### Added

- **Mention someone, and the event suggests adding them.** When a note or a
  voice memo on an event names someone from your People page, the event's AI
  suggests adding them to who was there — one tap to confirm, which also puts
  the event on their timeline. Dismiss it and they are not suggested for that
  event again.

### Changed

- **Suggestions on an event say why.** Under each suggestion you now see the
  words it came from, or its notes, instead of a technical name.
