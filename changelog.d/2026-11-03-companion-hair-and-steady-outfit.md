### Fixed
- **On "Surprise me" the companion changed clothes while you looked at
  her.** Opening a task could show her in one outfit and, a moment later,
  in another, because every redraw of the screen picked again. She now
  wears one outfit per task, goal, day or conversation from the moment it
  opens, keeps it for as long as the app runs, and wears the same one when
  you tap her to see her full screen. Another task may still show another
  outfit, and the next start of the app picks afresh.

### Changed
- **Nicer curls, afro and locs for the companion.** The curls and the afro
  are now drawn as soft, shaded coils with a rounded outline, instead of a
  flat cap stamped with little hooks, and the afro no longer shows a dark
  band beside her face. The locs sweep back from her hairline as twisted
  ropes instead of fanning out from one point at the top of her head.
