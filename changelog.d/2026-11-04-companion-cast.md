### Added
- **The companion can be a grandpa.** With Companion on, Settings ›
  Companion has a new Character choice: Lotti, or a grandpa in a shirt with
  rolled sleeves, braces, a bow tie, pleated trousers and brogues, with
  half-moon reading glasses and a trimmed beard. He has four outfits of his
  own (everyday, jazz, seaside, and market day with a flat cap), and
  "Surprise me" and the outfit button on the full-screen stage pick among
  them. Skin tone, hair colour and sass carry over when you switch.
- **The companion can be an aunt.** A third character in the same choice:
  a woman of about forty in a boiler suit with a belt and a chest pocket,
  short turned-up sleeves, high-top trainers and gold hoops, with fuller
  brows and no glasses. Her four outfits are everyday, studio, festival
  (with a headband) and weekend.
- **The companion can be an uncle.** A fourth character: a man of about
  thirty in a bomber jacket worn open over a tee, chinos turned up over
  lace-up boots, and headphones resting round his neck. His four outfits
  are everyday, night out, skate park, and snow day with a beanie.
- **Two more hairstyles for the companion: short and balding.** Every
  character can wear them, and the hairstyle picker now shows its choices as
  buttons that wrap, so every style fits on a phone.
- **The companion's eyes have a colour of their own.** They match the skin
  tone by default — no more blue eyes on every tone — and Settings ›
  Companion lets you pick dark brown, brown, hazel, amber, green, grey or
  blue.
- **A seventh, deeper skin tone for the companion,** after the six, and
  three more hairstyles for every character: long and straight, soft waves,
  and a ponytail.

### Fixed
- **A summary read aloud stopped when you switched to another app.** On a
  phone the voice now keeps speaking in the background, other apps' sound
  ducks under it, and it comes back when the voice ends. Like any app that
  speaks, it now plays with the iPhone's silent switch on.
- **The companion's hats sat inside an afro.** The rock beret, the flat cap
  and the beanie now sit on top of the hair, as wide as the hairstyle needs.
- **One raised brow could hook up off the companion's eye.** A brow now
  rises only as far as a brow can.
- **The bun looked like a grey loaf in dark hair colours.** Its knot now
  shows beside her jaw, and the hair reads as hair in every colour.
