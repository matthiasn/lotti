### Added
- **ElevenLabs voices can read aloud, the companion's included.** Add
  ElevenLabs under Settings → AI with your API key: its read-aloud models
  appear in a profile's *Read aloud* slot. Summaries, answers, the Day briefing
  and the companion then speak in an ElevenLabs voice. Speech settings list the
  account's voices, premade and your own, under each language; tap one to hear
  it read a sample sentence, and pick a different voice per language. Picks
  for Mistral and ElevenLabs are kept apart, so trying one never changes the
  other. Each preview uses about 60 characters of your ElevenLabs quota.
