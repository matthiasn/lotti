### Changed
- **Lotti now walks you through setup herself.** The welcome is her
  introducing herself in a speech bubble, and it says plainly what you need to
  get started: one API key. Before the key, you can make her yours — pick
  her outfit, skin tone, hair and hair colour and watch her change — or skip
  it; it all stays in Settings › Companion. The key step leads with a single recommended
  card, Mistral, whose one key covers thinking, transcription and her voice;
  the other providers are one tap away under "Other providers". She stays on
  hand for your first task, whether or not you have turned the companion on
  elsewhere in the app.
