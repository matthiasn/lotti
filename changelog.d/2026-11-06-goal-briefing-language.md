### Added
- **Goal briefings in your language.** *Edit goal* has a *Briefing language*
  under your agent's name. Left on *Automatic*, the agent writes its briefings,
  reports and banners in the default language of the goal's category, and
  follows it when that changes. Pick a language and it stays. If your journal's
  language later moves away from it, the page offers to switch.

### Fixed
- **Goal briefings came in English whatever language you journal in.** A goal
  without a language of its own now follows its category's language, or the
  language of your own words when the category names none.
- **The goal and person editors had smaller Save and Cancel buttons than the
  rest of the app.** Their bottom bar now uses the app's standard sticky action
  bar, with the same button size and glass background as the other editors.
