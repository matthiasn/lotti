### Added
- **The companion tells you what you completed.** Ask "what did I complete
  today?" or "what did I finish or reject in the past week?" and she lists
  the tasks you marked done or rejected in that window, saying which was
  which. A task counts on the day you finished it, even when you created
  it months ago, and one you reopened and finished again counts on its
  latest completion. Name a category to hear only that one.
