### Added
- **Ask the companion for a follow-up to a task you just talked about.**
  After she lists or names a task, say "create a follow-up to that one,
  high priority, due today, I started five minutes ago", or "add a
  follow-up to the task about the budget". She works out which task you
  mean from the conversation, finished tasks included, and asks when two
  could fit. One tap on *Make it a task* creates the follow-up in the same
  category, filled in, with its timer running from the time you gave. The
  new task shows *Follows up on* the old one, which shows *Has follow-up*,
  and the category's agent starts on it right away.
