### Added
- **Ask the companion for a task and she makes it, filled in and started.**
  Say "make a task to fix the gate, high priority, due Friday, this is for
  home, I started ten minutes ago". She sets the title, what to do,
  category, priority, due day and estimate from your words. When you say
  the work has begun, the task is in progress and its timer runs from the
  time you gave. Your words are kept on the task and the category's agent
  is assigned. She tells you she made it, and *Undo* takes it back. A task
  you only mention still waits in the inbox until you tap *Make it a task*,
  and that tap now fills it in the same way. When an open task already
  looks like the same work, she asks which one you mean instead of making
  a second.
