### Added
- **A task you dictate to the companion comes ready to confirm, filled in.**
  Say "make a task to fix the gate, high priority, due Friday, this is for
  home, I started ten minutes ago". She reads the title, what to do,
  category, priority, due day and estimate from your words and tells you
  what she understood. One tap on *Make it a task* creates it with all of
  that. It is in progress with its timer running from the time you gave
  only if you said the work had started. Otherwise it stays open. Your
  words are kept on the task and the category's agent is assigned. When an
  open task already looks like the same work, she asks which one you mean
  instead of proposing a second.
