### Added
- **Ask the companion about your pull requests.** "How many PRs did I create
  in the past 14 days?" or "What did I ship this weekend?" gets an answer such
  as "You created 12 PRs adding 1,450 lines and deleting 320 lines", with
  charts under it: pull requests, lines added and lines deleted per day, or as
  a running total. Any window up to a month works — the past 7, 14 or 30 days,
  this week, last month, or two dates you name. The numbers come from the pull
  requests already linked to your tasks on this device, so nothing is fetched
  from GitHub to answer, and with your GitHub account connected only the pull
  requests you opened count.
