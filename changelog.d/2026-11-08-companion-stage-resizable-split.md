### Changed
- **The conversation gets more room beside the companion's full-screen stage,
  and you can move the line between them.** On a desktop window she used to
  take three fifths of the width, though she stands portrait and needs far
  less. The conversation now takes three fifths by default, and the divider
  between them drags like the app's own columns — with the mouse or the arrow
  keys — and the app remembers where you left it.
