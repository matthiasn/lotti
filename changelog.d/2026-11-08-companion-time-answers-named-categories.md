### Fixed
- **Asking the companion about time in one category no longer shows all
  your other categories.** "What did I work on in Work this week?" used to
  draw a chart, and give an answer, that broke out every category you
  logged time in. Now only the category you named appears by name, and
  everything else is a single "Other" slice in both her words and the
  chart.
- **The ⋯ menus on chats, goals and people highlight their first and last
  row cleanly.** Hovering the top or bottom entry no longer leaves a strip
  of empty padding above or below the highlight.

### Added
- **The companion tells you which tasks your time went to.** Ask "what did
  I work on in the past seven days?" and she lists the tasks you logged
  time on in that window as task rows, along with the totals and the
  chart. For a week, she also says in one sentence which category took
  most of your time.
- **Task rows in the companion's answers show each task's priority.** Due,
  overdue and other task lists in her replies carry the P0–P3 glyph and
  label the tasks list uses, so the urgent ones stand out.
