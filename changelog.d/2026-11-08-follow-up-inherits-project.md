### Fixed
- **A follow-up you ask the companion for lands in its task's project.**
  "Create a follow-up to that one" linked the new task to the one it
  follows but left it out of every project, so it was missing from that
  project's task list until you filed it by hand. It now joins the same
  project, and stays in none when the task it follows has none.

### Changed
- **The companion picks a task's category from the conversation.** Ask
  for a task without naming a category, and when everything you have
  been talking about, or all the time you logged today, sits in one
  category, the new task goes there. She says which category she chose,
  so you can change it before you confirm. When it could be either, she
  still leaves it for you.
