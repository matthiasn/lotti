### Changed
- **One input for typing and talking, everywhere.** Every chat — the
  companion, an agent's chat, a goal's or a person's — and the Daily OS
  composer now share one input. Your words use its full width and wrap onto
  new lines, so a long dictation is never cut off under a button.
- **Read it before it goes.** When you finish talking, tap ✓: your words
  appear in the field, after anything you already typed, so you can read and
  correct them before sending. Nothing is sent until you press send.
- **The mic stays.** The mic is always there, even once you have typed
  something, so you can switch between typing and talking at any time.
- **Name a category with @.** In the companion chat, typing @ suggests your
  categories; pick one to narrow the answer to it.

### Fixed
- **The recording bars filled only half of a wide input.** While you talk,
  the bars now run across the whole input within a few seconds, on a phone
  and on the widest window.
