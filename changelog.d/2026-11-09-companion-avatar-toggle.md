### Changed
- **Tapping the companion's face now shows its card right in the
  chat.** It used to open a separate full-screen view; now the glass card
  fades in over your conversation, with the chat, its header and the input
  staying where they are, and a tap on the card puts it away again. The face
  in the chat header is a little larger, in a rounded tile, and steps aside
  while the card is out; the conversation's title gets the full width under
  the controls.
