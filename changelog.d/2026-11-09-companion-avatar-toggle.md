### Changed
- **Tapping the companion's face now shows or hides its card right in the
  chat.** It used to open a separate full-screen view; now the glass card
  fades in over your conversation and a second tap fades it out, with the
  chat, its header and the input staying where they are. The face in the
  chat header is larger, and the conversation's title gets the full width
  under the controls, wrapping to a second line before it is cut off.
