### Added
- **A task the companion makes now shows up in her chat as a preview card.**
  When a turn of yours becomes a task, her reply carries a compact card for
  it: its cover or status, its title and one-line summary, its category, when
  it is due and how long it is estimated to take. The card follows the task
  as it changes after the chat, and a tap opens the task on top of the
  conversation. A task the card already shows is not listed a second time in
  her reply.
