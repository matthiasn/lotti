### Added
- **What the companion makes for you now shows up in her chat as a preview
  card.** When a turn of yours becomes a task, her reply carries a compact
  card for it: its cover or status, its title and one-line summary, its
  category, when it is due and how long it is estimated to take. The card
  follows the task as it changes after the chat, and a tap opens the task on
  top of the conversation. A task the card already shows is not listed a
  second time in her reply.
- **Make an event from the companion's inbox.** With Events turned on,
  something nothing holds yet can now become an event with *Make it an
  event*, titled with your words.
  Her reply shows it as the same event card the rest of the app uses — its
  cover, rating, category, date, status and summary, kept current — and a
  tap opens the event's page. *Undo* takes the event back out, and the item
  waits in the inbox again.
- **The companion's lists of tasks are preview cards now, under a label
  that says what they are.** When she answers what is due, each task she
  names is the same card: its cover or status, one-line summary, category,
  due date, estimate and priority, kept current. A quiet label over the list
  says which window she read — *Due today*, *Overdue*, *Due through Fri,
  Nov 13* — and a project she lists is a card that opens it.
- **A day she helped you replan shows its plan.** After you take a change to
  today into the Daily OS planning and accept it, her reply shows that day's
  blocks — when each runs, what it is for, the done ones ticked — and a tap
  opens the day.
- **Projects show up in the companion's chat as cards too.** A project you
  make from her inbox, or one she lists, is drawn as the Projects list draws
  it — its progress, task count and status — kept current, and a tap opens
  its page.
- **Long-press a task card in her chat for quick actions.** *Change status…*,
  *Reschedule…* and *Move to project…* open the same pickers as the task's own
  page, without leaving the conversation. A right-click opens them on desktop.

### Fixed
- **Asking the companion for a task "in progress" no longer starts a timer.**
  The task is made in progress, and a timer runs only when you ask for one
  or say when you started.
- **What you say about a new task stays with it.** "Create a task to pack.
  Make it due today and urgent." is one task in her inbox, not two items.
