### Changed
- **The companion sums logged time for any days you ask about.** "How much did
  I log in the past 14 days?" or "from June 1st to June 5th" used to fall back
  to a fixed span such as the past seven days. She now sums exactly the days you
  name, up to a year, and the chart under her answer shows those days: one bar
  per day, or one per week for more than 120 days. A range from another year is
  titled with its year.
