### Changed
- **Asking the companion where your time went gets a short summary first.**
  "What did I do in the past 14 days?" is now answered in a few sentences you
  can listen to: the total, your categories and biggest projects, and your
  busiest day, instead of every task one by one. She offers to break it
  down, and when you ask — "show me the Lotti tasks by duration", "what did
  I do on Tuesday?" — she lists the tasks, in the order you ask for.
