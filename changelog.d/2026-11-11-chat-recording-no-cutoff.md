### Fixed
- **A spoken message in a chat is no longer cut off after two minutes.**
  The recorder behind every chat — the companion's, a task's, a goal's, a
  person's — stopped itself at 120 seconds and transcribed what it had, so a
  longer thought arrived in two halves and the second had to be recorded
  again. It now records until you stop it, as a recording on a task does.
