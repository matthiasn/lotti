### Fixed
- **A list of tasks in the companion's answer is cards.** When she wrote a
  list of task links under her words, it showed as plain links. Such a
  list is now drawn as cards.
- **The evolution chat no longer shows a card the agent didn't ask for.**
  When the agent asked for a card type that doesn't exist, or left out
  what a card needs, the chat showed an empty card. Now nothing is drawn,
  and the agent is told why so it can try again.

### Added
- **Replies you can tap.** When the companion offers something, such as
  breaking a time summary down by task, she can show short replies to tap
  in place of typing.
- **Time on task cards.** When she breaks logged time down by task, each
  task's card shows its time.
