### Changed
- **The companion chat has a cleaner header and a side drawer.** The header
  is now one row: the way to your chats, the companion's face and name, a
  new chat, and close. A small dot on the chats button says something waits
  in your inbox; how many is shown in the drawer. Your chats, their
  search, a new chat and the companion's settings — spoken answers, her AI
  profile and her internals, with what the open chat has cost — now live in
  a drawer that slides over the conversation on a phone and sits beside it
  on a wider window. Opening the drawer no longer takes the conversation off
  the screen, and no longer stops a message you are recording.
- **Pick a category for a chat right where you type.** A chip at the start
  of the message field shows the chat's category, or "Everything". Pick one
  and every question you send in that chat is answered from that category
  only; typing @ still narrows a single question. Each chat in the drawer
  shows its category's colour, and the drawer lists the categories your
  chats are in, so you can show only the chats in one.
- **The conversation shows only what was said.** The per-answer cost line
  under the companion's replies is gone, the hint under the message field is
  replaced by the category chip, and your own messages sit in a quieter,
  neutral bubble.
