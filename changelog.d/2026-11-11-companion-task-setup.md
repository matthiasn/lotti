### Changed
- **Set up a task before the companion makes it.** Tapping "Make it a task"
  under something you told the companion now asks how, right in the chat:
  its status, priority and due date as three chips, already set to what she
  understood from your words, under the title she will give the task. Urgent
  shows red and a task due today shows orange, as they will in your task
  list. Change any of them with a tap — the due date offers today, tomorrow,
  a day on the calendar or none — then create the task as you set it up, or
  press Enter.
- Menus no longer open flush against the edge of the screen.
