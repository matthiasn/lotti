### Changed
- **The companion says each thing once, and her cards and charts come with every answer.**
  Her answer used to be assembled from two places for words, so a sentence
  could be shown twice and read aloud twice. She now writes one answer: what
  is on the screen first, then what she says about it. Short labels between
  cards are shown but not spoken, and cards and charts carry a description
  for screen readers.

### Fixed
- **A question about what is overdue or due was now and then handed on instead of answered.**
  After looking the tasks up, the companion could route the question to
  another agent and leave it without an answer. She now answers it herself.
