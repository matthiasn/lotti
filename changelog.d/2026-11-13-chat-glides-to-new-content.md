### Fixed
- **Chats no longer jump when a chart or long answer arrives.** Every new reply,
  card or chart snapped the conversation to the bottom the moment it was laid
  out, often while you were still reading. New content now lands without moving
  what you are looking at, and the thread then glides down to it at reading
  pace. Touching, clicking, dragging or scrolling stops the glide right where it
  is, and a reply that arrives while you are scrolled up leaves you in place.
