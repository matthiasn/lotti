### Added
- **Cover art and coding prompts from the companion chat.** A task made in
  her chat could not have either: both lived in the AI menu of a recording on
  the task page. Now *Make it a task* asks one more thing beside status,
  priority and due day — a **Cover art** chip, off unless you asked for one
  ("…with cover art") — and the cover is drawn from your words once the task
  is made. The item's menu gains **Task with a coding prompt**, which makes
  the task as she read it and has a coding prompt written from the same
  words. Typed or dictated makes no difference: both work from the text you
  gave her, no recording needed. The card says what was requested, and the
  result appears on the task when it is ready.
  The chip appears when the task's category, or your default AI profile, has
  an image model to draw with.
- **The cover and the coding prompt show up in the chat.** The task's card
  under her reply says when a cover is being drawn or a prompt written, then
  shows the cover as its thumbnail and the prompt's summary under the task —
  so you see both before opening it. A tap opens the task with the full
  prompt.

### Changed
- **The task setup's chips read as one group.** On a phone the second row of
  chips sat far below the first, as if it were something else. The chips are
  now larger, evenly spaced in a two-by-two block, and always break as
  status and priority, then due day and cover. Values you did not set step
  back, so what she heard or you chose stands out, and Cancel and Create task
  are easier to hit and further apart.

### Fixed
- **Cover art and prompts could not be generated for a task in no
  category.** Such a task has no AI profile of its own, and *Generate Cover
  Art* and the prompt generators quietly did nothing for it. They now run on
  your default AI profile, as the companion does.
