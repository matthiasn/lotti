### Changed
- **A task with time tracked on it is suggested as in progress.** When a task is
  still open but you have recorded time on it, or you dictate a new task along
  with the time you spent, the task agent now suggests moving it to *In
  Progress*. The suggestion sits with the other status changes for you to
  confirm on its own, and it isn't made when the task notes say the work is
  blocked or on hold.
