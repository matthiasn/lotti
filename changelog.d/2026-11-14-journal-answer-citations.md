- **Answers from your journal showed blank dots where they cited a source.**
  Each citation is now a readable number that opens the entry it names, and the
  cited entries sit under a *Sources* heading of their own, named by what they
  are and when instead of by a recording's file name.

  you ask for, in short paragraphs, with every jab pointing at the entry it
  comes from. It teases your habits, never your health, body, grief, money
  worries or the people around you.
