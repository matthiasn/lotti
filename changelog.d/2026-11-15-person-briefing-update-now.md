### Changed
- **A person's briefing card offers Update now where you can see it.** A
  current briefing kept its refresh behind the card's ⋯ menu; it now sits under
  the briefing the way it does on a task's card, reading *Thinking…* while a new
  briefing is written, with the model and provider it will use named right
  beneath it. Tapping the card's header still opens the agent's activity.

### Fixed
- **A person's timeline no longer confuses two headings for the same month.** A
  meeting called off later this month put that month's heading above *Today* as
  well as below *This week*, and the list could take one for the other while it
  scrolled. A check-in with more than one recording also now always leads with
  the summary of the recording it plays, rather than whichever one loaded first.
