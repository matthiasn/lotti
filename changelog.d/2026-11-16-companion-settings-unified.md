### Changed
- **The companion's look and voice are set in one place.** Her settings were
  one long scroll in which she slid out of view after the first choice, and
  her voice lived on a different page under Speech. Settings › Companion now
  keeps her in sight — over the choices on a phone, beside them on a wide
  window — and sorts the choices into three tabs: **Look**, **Voice** and
  **Personality**. The Voice tab holds everything Speech held: whether she
  answers aloud, the on-device voice, the cloud voices, the reading speed
  and, under Advanced, the speech model. **Hear me** has her say a line in
  the on-device voice you chose, her mouth moving with it. While the companion is on,
  Speech no longer has its own entry in Settings.
- **Her voice follows who she is.** Choosing the grandpa or the uncle switches
  to a male on-device voice, Lotti or the aunt to a female one, keeping the
  number you had. Once you pick a voice yourself it stays your pick, whoever
  she is.

### Fixed
- **Colour swatches no longer leave one stranded on a second line.** The
  hair, skin and eye colours are shared out evenly when they need two rows.
