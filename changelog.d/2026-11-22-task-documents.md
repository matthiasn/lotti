### Added
- **Tasks can hold documents.** "Write a document" in a task's Add sheet
  creates a markdown document on the task, such as a client one-pager, a
  meeting summary or a follow-up email. The task's new Documents section
  lists each one with its version and when it was last written. Open one to
  read it, edit it, copy its markdown out to paste elsewhere, or delete it.
  Every save keeps the earlier text as a version: History lists them, and
  any older one can be restored. An edit never overwrites a newer version
  saved meanwhile on another device.
