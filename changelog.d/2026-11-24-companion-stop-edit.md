### Added
- **You can stop the companion while she works on a message.** While she
  is answering, the send button in the chat becomes **Stop**. A stopped
  message stays in the conversation marked *Stopped*. She doesn't answer
  it, and no other device picks it up later.
- **Copy and edit your own messages.** On a phone, long-press one of your
  messages for a small window with **Copy** and **Edit**, headed by when
  you sent it. Edit puts the words back in the chat box so you can fix them
  and send them again: the edited message and her new answer take the place
  of the old message and her old answer. Anything you had already typed is
  kept: arrows in the chat box flip back to it.
  Long-pressing a message she is still working on tells you to stop it
  first, with a **Stop** button right there. On a desktop, the same actions
  appear as small icons beside your message when you point at it.
