# Talk to your companion

The companion is the place to start. Instead of opening the right page first,
you ask her — in writing or aloud — and she finds the answer: what is due
today, how much time you logged this week, how a project is going. When you
tell her something, she works out whether it belongs to a task, a project, a
goal or today's plan, and proposes filing it there. Nothing is written until
you tap.

The detail pages stay where they are. The chat is where things start; tasks,
projects, goals and the Daily OS are where you refine what was started —
estimates, checklists, priorities, blocks on the timeline.

:::note[Experimental]

The companion chat is new and still growing. It ships switched off, and what
she can do changes from release to release. This page describes what works
today; [What is coming](#what-is-coming) lists what is planned.

:::

## Turn her on

Open **Settings → Advanced Settings → Config Flags**. In the **Advanced &
experimental** group, turn on:

| Switch | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Companion chat** | The chat itself. Required. |
| **Companion** | Her animated figure in the chat, beside AI summaries and in the Day header, plus **Settings → Preferences → Companion** to dress her. Optional. |
| **AI summary playback** (in the Preferences group) | Her voice. Needed, together with **Companion**, for her to answer aloud. |

The chat needs a working AI provider. She uses the **Settings default** AI
profile unless you pick another one for her, and falls back to a small hosted
model from Melious or Mistral when you have set up one of those providers. See
[Connect an AI provider](../ai-and-automation/provider-setup.mdx).

## Open the chat

- **Desktop:** select **Ask the companion** in the sidebar, or press
  **Cmd+L** (macOS) / **Ctrl+L** (Linux and Windows) from anywhere.
- **Phone:** tap **Ask the companion** beside the menu button in the
  navigation bar.

While the app is locked to a single category, she is hidden, because her
conversations span every category.

## Ask her

Type in **Ask anything…** and send. Questions she answers today:

- **What is due.** "What's due today?", "What's overdue?", "What's due this
  week?" She keeps today and overdue apart unless you ask for both.
- **Where your time went.** "How much time did I log this week?" works for
  today, yesterday, this week, the past 7 or 30 days, this month and last
  month. She sums time per category with the same numbers as
  [Time analysis](../organize-and-reflect/time-analysis.mdx), shows a chart, and
  names the tasks the time went to when you ask what you worked on.
- **What you finished.** "What did I complete this week?" counts each task on
  the day it was completed or rejected, not the day it was created.
- **A specific task or project.** "Where does the habitat redesign stand?" She
  hands the question to that task's or project's agent and shows its answer
  under its name — **From …** — without rewording it.
- **A goal.** A question or update about one of your goals becomes a turn in
  that goal agent's own chat, and its reply appears in yours. Goals are an
  optional section: turn on **Goals** under **Settings → Sections**.
- **Your journal.** "What did we decide about the sardine supplier?" When no
  single task or project holds the answer, she searches your journal and
  answers **From your journal**, listing the entries she relied on. Each one
  opens its source.
- **Your pull requests.** "How many pull requests did I merge this week?" works
  when GitHub is connected under **Settings → Advanced Settings → GitHub**. She
  counts only the pull requests already stored on this device.

Two or more tasks come back as a card of rows — status, the agent's one-line
summary, category, priority and due date. Tap a task to open it on top of the
chat; going back returns you to the conversation.

Type **@** to name a category and narrow the answer to it. She only ever sees
the categories she is allowed to see.

If she is not sure what you meant, she asks one short question with up to
three choices.

## Tell her what happened

When you tell her something instead of asking, she proposes where it goes. A
proposal is a card with buttons; nothing is filed until you tap.

| You say | She proposes |
| --- | --- |
| An update about a task or project | **File it** — your exact words become an entry under it. |
| "Make a task to fix the gate, high priority" | **Make it a task** — she fills in the title, category, priority, due day, estimate and start time from what you said and tells you what she read. The task's category agent is assigned. |
| "A follow-up to that one" | **Make it a task**, linked as a follow-up to the task named earlier in the conversation. |
| "Move the count to the afternoon" | **Change today** — opens [Daily OS](../plan-and-capture/daily-os.mdx) planning with your words. |
| Something new and bigger | **New project…** or **Start a goal…**, opening the form with your words in it. |
| Anything else | **Save as a note**, or **Leave it**. |

If she guessed the wrong place, choose **Somewhere else**. Anything nothing
owns waits in the **Inbox**, opened from its chip in the chat header or the
list of conversations.
Everything a tap wrote can be opened or undone.

## Keep several conversations

Like a ChatGPT or Claude window, each subject can have its own conversation —
start one with **New chat**.
Conversations are titled by their first question and listed newest first
behind the **Chats** button in the header (beside the conversation on a wide
desktop window). Use **Search chats** to find one, and each row's menu to
rename, archive or delete it. Deleting hides a conversation on every device;
anything you filed from it stays where it is.

## Talk to her

Ask a question aloud and she answers aloud, with her lips moving to her
voice. A typed question always gets a written answer. Tap to talk and tap
again to send, or hold to talk and let go to send.

Speaking needs **Companion** and **AI summary playback** on, and a voice:
either the read-aloud model of her AI profile, or the on-device voice where
your platform supports it. In the header you can mute her, have her **Say it
again**, or choose **View companion full screen** to see her full size. On a
wide desktop window the conversation sits beside her there, and she points at
each task she lists as she says it.

To pick the AI profile she thinks with, open **AI profile** in the header
menu. **Settings default** makes her follow your default again.

## Make her yours

With **Companion** on, open **Settings → Preferences → Companion**:

- **Character:** Lotti (a grandmother), Grandpa, Aunt or Uncle.
- **Skin tone, Hair color, Hairstyle, Eye color** and **Outfit** — or a fresh
  outfit each time you open the app.
- **Sass:** Warm, Cheeky or Sassy — how she reacts to what she reads aloud.
- **Voice:** **Answer spoken questions aloud** turns her spoken replies on or
  off.

Besides the chat, she stands beside a task's AI summary and reads it when you
press its read-aloud control, sits in the Day page header and briefs you on
the day when tapped, and greets you during onboarding.

## Chat first, detail pages for the details

| Start in the chat | Refine on the page |
| --- | --- |
| "What's due today?" | [Tasks](../organize-and-reflect/tasks.mdx) — status, priority, due date, checklists |
| "Make a task to…" | The new task — estimate, labels, checklist, linked entries |
| "How much time this week?" | [Time analysis](../organize-and-reflect/time-analysis.mdx) — any range, any category |
| "Move X to the afternoon" | [Daily OS](../plan-and-capture/daily-os.mdx) — blocks on the timeline |
| An update on a project | [Projects](../organize-and-reflect/projects.mdx) — the project's report and tasks |

## What is coming \{#what-is-coming\}

Everything below is direction, not a shipped feature; scope and order can
change.

- **People.** Asking about and telling her about the people in your life,
  handed to each person's agent the way goals already are.
- **Events.** Asking about an event and filing photos and notes to it from the
  chat.
- **Settings and customization by asking.** Changing how Lotti behaves — or
  how she looks — by saying so, instead of finding the right settings page.
- **Filing without the tap,** as an opt-in, for the kinds of updates you
  always confirm.
- **More of her.** Moods that reflect your record, gentle nudges, and a face
  for each agent.

## Privacy

She reads only what is already on your device, within the categories she may
see. One question can use more than one AI profile: her own profile decides
where the question goes, a question about a task, project or goal is answered
with that agent's profile, and an answer from your journal uses the
**Settings default** profile. Your question and the parts of your record each
answer needs go to the provider of the profile used. To keep everything on
your device, give all of them a local model. She never changes your record on
her own: every write is a tap.
