# Work toward goals

A goal is something you keep working toward over weeks or months: walking
more, keeping your blood pressure under control, writing in a journal every
day. Each goal has its own agent. Lotti measures the signals you chose — habit
completions, steps, health readings, measurables, tracked time, time spent
checking in — and works out at no AI cost whether you are on track. The agent
then writes a briefing about where things stand and what comes next, and
answers what you tell it in your check-ins.

Goals are an optional section. Open **Settings → Sections** and turn on
**Goals**. The **Goals** destination then appears in the desktop sidebar and
in the navigation launcher on mobile. Habits that belong to a goal show inside
it; habits that do not are listed under **Not in a goal**.

## Create a goal

Select the **+** button on the Goals page — or **New goal agent** while you
have none — and follow three steps:

1. **What do you want to work toward?** Say it your way, for example "Get to
   the gym twice a week, and keep up my morning exercises." This is how the
   agent will talk about the goal.
2. **Here's what I can watch.** Lotti suggests the signals your words point
   to. **Add a signal** opens one picker for the rest — habits, the automatic
   step count, health readings such as weight and blood pressure, your
   measurables, tracked time and **Check-in time**. Each signal gets its
   target, and the goal gets its **Goal name**. When nothing observable matches, the agent says so instead of
   pretending: pick a signal it can see or rewrite the intention.
3. **Meet your agent.** Give the agent an **Agent name**, choose the goal's
   category and select **Create agent**.

The agent can only coach what it can see. It is told only about the signals
listed under **Signals** on the goal page.

### Goals whose check-ins are the point

Some goals are about what you say rather than whether you said it — a journal,
for example. **I'll answer what you tell me** tells the agent to read your
check-ins for what they say. Left alone, the switch follows whether the goal
measures **Check-in time**.

## Edit a goal

Open the goal's **⋮** menu and choose **Edit goal**. Editing is one page:

- **Your goal** — the statement **In your words**, the **Goal name** and the
  category, under a line naming the version the save starts (**This starts
  version 3. Your history is kept.**, for example);
- the goal's signals and their targets; and
- **Your agent** — **I'll answer what you tell me**, the **Agent name** and
  the **Briefing language**.

**Save new version** keeps the earlier versions as history; **Cancel** leaves
the goal as it was. Leaving with unsaved changes asks first.

**Briefing language** set to **Automatic** writes the briefings, reports and
banners in the default language of the goal's category and follows it when it
changes. A language you pick stays. If your journal later moves to another
language, the page offers to switch to it.

The same menu offers **Update now**, **Automatic updates**, **Agent
internals** and **Delete goal**. To move a goal to another category, select
the category above its title; everything the goal holds — check-ins,
recordings, notes and **About this goal** — moves with it.

## Check in

A check-in is you telling the goal what is actually going on, in your own
words, whenever it is worth saying. Select **Record a check-in** from the
goal's header or its **Check-ins** card to start the recorder; **Write
instead** opens a text field. Typed check-ins end with **Save check-in**.

Every check-in is an ordinary journal entry, filed in the goal's category.
Spoken check-ins are transcribed with the goal agent's profile, else the
category's, else your default profile. A check-in also counts as time spent:
a recording counts its length, a written check-in its writing time, and every
row on the timeline shows its minutes so you can correct them.

Check-ins and your daily reflections share one timeline. On a phone the
**Check-ins** card previews the latest rows, and **See all check-ins** opens
the whole history; on a wide window the timeline sits beside the goal. Goal
check-ins look and behave like a person's: the same rows, a spinner with
**Transcribing…** while words are on their way, and **Show more** for long
text.

To judge a day yourself, use **Reflect on today**. The sheet suggests a
verdict from what was measured, which you can change.

## Read the agent's briefing and report

Each goal's agent writes a daily briefing on its morning update, as long as
**Automatic updates** is on. The goal page shows its read under the goal
definition, in sections such as **Where things stand**, **From your
check-ins** and **What's next**. The Goals page opens with a **Today** card:
every goal's one-line read and its first next step, goals that are behind
first.

**From your check-ins** answers what you said. It names the days it answers —
**About your check-ins on …** — and check-ins it has not answered yet wait for
the next report. A check-in or habit you record marks the report out of date;
**Update now** writes a new one immediately. Recorded check-ins never change
the measured status, which comes from the signals alone.

With the **Companion** switch on, the companion stands beside the goal's
summary. Tap her to see her full screen (**View companion full screen**).

## Tell the agent what matters

- **About this goal** holds what the goal is for and how the agent should
  coach you — "ask me one guided question each morning". Write or record it
  with **Write about this goal** or **Record about this goal**. The agent
  reads it every time it writes to you.
- **Talk to agent** opens the goal's chat. Tell it "I only walk after dinner"
  and it keeps that as a fact. What it only inferred waits under **What I've
  learned** for you to confirm, edit or forget.
- When a check-in leaves something open, the agent may ask one question in
  the chat, at most once a day and never while its last one is unanswered. A
  banner can say a question is waiting.
- Notes the agent marks as critical, such as a doctor's advice, stay in view
  however much is written after them.

Ask the [companion](../getting-started/companion.mdx) about a goal and her
question becomes a turn in that goal's chat. Habits and measurables a goal
watches are set up as described in
[Habits and measurables](habits-and-measurables.mdx).
