# Stay close to people

People is a small, deliberately curated list of the people you want to stay
close to. Each person has a timeline of your check-ins — how you connected,
how it felt, what you talked about — together with notes, voice notes,
photos, tasks, meetings and events linked to them. For someone you turn on
reminders for, an agent tracks how long it has been and prepares a briefing
from your check-ins.

People is an optional section. Open **Settings → Sections** and turn on
**People**. The **People** destination then appears in the desktop sidebar
and in the navigation launcher on mobile.

## Browse the People list

The list groups people under **Due**, **On track** and **No reminders**, below
a summary of who is due now and who is due next. Each row shows when you last
spoke and, where there is one, how often a reminder comes. On desktop the
selected person opens beside the list; on a phone the person opens as a page
of their own. A long press on a person starts speaking about them without
opening the page.

## Add or edit a person

Select **Add person** to open the person editor, or **Import from contacts**
on Android and iOS to pick people from the device's contacts. To edit
someone, open the **⋮** menu on their page and choose **Edit person**. The
editor is a page like the goal editor, in sections:

- **Who** — the category, **Name**, **Nickname** and **Birthday**. The
  person's agent briefs with the AI profile this category names, or with your
  default profile, unless you choose an AI setup for that agent yourself.
- **How to reach them** — phone numbers, messaging and email, added with
  **Add channel**. Contact channels stay on this device and never reach the
  AI.
- **Your agent** — **Remind me to stay in touch** and **How often?**, the
  person's **Status**, and **Names that come up**: people, pets and places you
  mention with them, so dictated check-ins spell them your way.
- **Photo** — the face and an optional banner image. Photo changes save right
  away and the agent never sees them.

**Cancel** and **Save** — **Create** for someone new — sit along the bottom,
and saving opens the person's page. Leaving with unsaved changes
asks first. If the person was deleted meanwhile — here or on another device —
the editor says **This person is no longer tracked.** instead of loading
forever.

The category above the person's name is one tap from the category picker.
Moving a person moves their check-ins, notes, voice notes, photos and
meetings with them; linked tasks keep their own category.

## Log a check-in

Select **Log check-in** from the page's **Add** menu. The check-in composer
opens on the note, with **Dictate** inside the field. Under it, set how you connected, when it
started, how long it took and how it felt. **Topics** and what to pay
attention to next time fold out below.

**Save check-in** saves it. A few words or a recording are enough: words
still being transcribed land on the recording after you save. When your words
say when, how long or how you connected, those facts are filled in for you
unless you already picked them, and every fact stays editable. How a
conversation felt is never filled in by the agent.

Dictation records against the person and transcribes with the first profile
that can: their agent's, then their category's, then your default profile.
Misheard names are corrected
against the person's name and nickname, **Names that come up**, the other
people in the category and the category's
[speech dictionary](speech-dictionary.mdx).

Goal and People check-ins look alike: the same timeline rows, one line saying
when and what, and a spoken check-in that shows **Transcribing…** until its
words arrive. Open a check-in to see it laid out like a task, with **Edit
check-in** in its **⋮** menu.

## Read the person's briefing

For someone with reminders on, the briefing card sits on their page. Its
status line says what the agent is doing: watching, writing the briefing, out
of date after a new check-in, or that the last run failed. The briefing
itself reads like a task's summary, with **Read more** to expand it and
**Open agent internals** beside it.

Every state of the card ends on the same single line, as on a task's card:
the model and provider on the left and **Update now** on the right —
**Thinking…** while a briefing is written. Tap the model row to choose
another model. **Update now** starts at once, because the model and provider
are already named beside it. While a new briefing is written, and after a
failed run, the previous briefing stays readable. Without reminders the card
offers **How often?** and **Turn on reminders** instead.

The agent can suggest tasks from commitments in your check-ins. Each
suggestion names the check-in it came from; nothing is created until you
confirm. The header's chat opens a conversation with the briefing agent
about the person — never with the person.

## Keep their timeline

The timeline runs newest first under **Upcoming**, **Today**, **This week** and
each month, and can be filtered by kind. The bar at the bottom offers
**Speak** and **Add**. **Add** holds a check-in, a meeting, a note (which can
be pinned to the person's **About** section), a task or photos. **Speak**
records whatever is on your mind and keeps it on the timeline as a voice note
at once; its words are then sorted into a check-in, a note, a meeting or a
task for you to review, and nothing is filed until you save. A voice note
that was not sorted offers **Sort this** on its row.

Events you share with someone appear on their timeline too. On an event's
page, add the person under **People**; the event then shows on that person's
timeline, and the chip on the event opens their page. See
[Events](events.mdx).

Deleting a person removes their check-ins, the notes, voice notes and photos
on their timeline, and their agent. Linked tasks and meetings are kept.
