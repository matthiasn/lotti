# Keep a speech dictionary

Speech recognition writes down what it hears. Names, places and project jargon
it has never met come out misspelled — **Project Waddle** as "Project Wattle".
The speech dictionary is the list of words Lotti should get right, kept once
for the whole app.

## Add a term

1. Open **Settings → Definitions → Speech dictionary**.
2. Select **Add term**, or type the word into the search field and choose
   **Add "…"** when nothing matches.
3. Enter the term exactly as it should be spelled.

You can also select a word in the text of any entry and choose **Add to
Dictionary**. It is added for that entry's category.

## Limit a term to categories

Use **Choose categories** to apply a term only to recordings in those
categories, so niche vocabulary stays out of unrelated transcripts. With none
chosen, the term applies to every category.

## Misheard as

List the spellings a term tends to come out as, separated by semicolons. Lotti
adds the ones it corrects, too. They are hints, never automatic replacements: a
misheard spelling can also be a real word the speaker meant, and the
surrounding text decides.

## How transcripts are corrected

When a recording in a task is transcribed by a speech-to-text engine such as
Whisper, the step that writes its one-liner and summary also corrects the
dictionary terms the transcript misheard. The recording's text appears once,
already corrected, and stays editable. A multimodal model that listens to the
audio itself reads the dictionary in its own prompt instead.

The terms your categories held before were carried over automatically, each
limited to the categories that held it.
