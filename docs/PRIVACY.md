# Lotti Privacy Policy

*Effective date: August 30, 2026*

## Overview

Lotti is a privacy-first personal productivity app developed by Matthias
Nehlsen and published by Nehlsen EDV Beratung GmbH. Neither Matthias Nehlsen
nor Nehlsen EDV Beratung GmbH collects, receives, stores, or has access to data
you enter or create in Lotti.

This policy reflects Lotti's App Store privacy declaration: **the developer
does not collect data from the app**.

## Data Storage

Your tasks, habits, tracked time, voice notes, journal entries, and other app
content are stored on your own devices. Lotti does not require a developer-run
account or upload this content to the developer. Nehlsen EDV Beratung GmbH
cannot view or retrieve it.

## Synchronization

The developer, Matthias Nehlsen, plans to offer synchronization between your
own devices. When you use synchronization, all synced content is end-to-end
encrypted (E2EE) before it leaves your device. Only your devices hold the keys
needed to decrypt the content. The developer, server operators, and other
intermediaries cannot read the synced content.

Servers used to relay or store synchronized data may handle encrypted data,
but they receive no readable copy of your app content.

## AI Inference

AI inference is optional. Lotti does not mandate or bundle a particular AI
inference provider; you choose and configure the provider, if any, that you
trust.

The only app content that leaves your devices in readable form is content you
explicitly send for AI inference, such as summarization. A local inference
provider can keep this processing on your own device. If you select a remote
provider, the content needed for the request is sent directly to that provider
and is governed by its privacy policy, security practices, and retention terms.
Selecting and using that provider is your choice and responsibility.

Nehlsen EDV Beratung GmbH does not receive or retain the content sent to your
chosen inference provider.

## Data Collection by the Developer

Lotti contains no developer-operated service that collects your app content.
The developer does not collect data from Lotti for analytics, advertising,
profiling, or other purposes. Optional end-to-end encrypted synchronization
does not give the developer access to readable content, and optional AI
requests go only to the provider you choose.

## Open Source Transparency

Lotti's source code is publicly available on
[GitHub](https://github.com/matthiasn/lotti), allowing its data-handling
implementation to be independently inspected.

## Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated if Lotti's features or data-handling practices
change. The effective date above will be updated when changes are published.
