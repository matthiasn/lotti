## Cost

Whole-run price from the billing relay, and the task-agent token profile that
explains it.

| Model | Score | Run price | Input €/M | Output €/M | Cached input | Output tokens/case |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| GLM 5.3 Flash | 466 | ≥ €1.04 | 0.20 | 0.51 | 80% | 516 |
| GLM 5.3 (control) | 474 | €3.26 | 1.00 | 3.04 | 98% | 424 |
| DeepSeek Flash | 475 | ≥ €4.17 | 0.50 | 1.52 | 54% | 1002 |
| **Kimi K3** | **454** | **€25.16** | **3.01** | **15.5** | 94% | 468 |

Per-token prices come from two controlled requests per model (a ~8k-token prompt
with a five-token answer, and a short prompt with a long answer). A `≥` price is
a run with a few unpriced requests, so the total is a known subtotal.

**Kimi K3 costs 7.7× the control and 24× GLM Flash, and scores lowest.** Its
cache behaviour is fine (94%) and its token use is close to the control's: the
price is simply its list price. It is not a candidate for any Lotti route.

Kimi's failures are also a different kind from the flash models'. It acts where
the others correctly do nothing — a forbidden tool call in all three
`undecided_evidence` samples, `gp_noop` mutated twice, five `goal-outcomes`
writes on a quiet wake — and it returned no assistant content at all in all
three `pv_no_contact_details` samples. Its reports, by contrast, are clean:
`task-workflow` 3/3 and `task-directives` 21/21, the two suites where the flash
models lose points to wording.

**DeepSeek costs more than the stronger control** despite half its per-token
price, for two reasons visible above: it writes 2.4× the output tokens per case
(mostly reasoning), and only 54% of its input is served from the provider's
prompt cache against the control's 98%. Part of that gap was the gym's own
scheduling, fixed in #4339 after these runs: samples of one exercise ran side by
side, so identical prompts all missed the cache. Every run here predates that
fix, so DeepSeek's price is overstated relative to a user's sequential wakes.

## Model misses

- **DeepSeek, 5.** `task-workflow` "no estimate or due date is set" (a phrase
  the report contract forbids, 6 of 51 samples across the day's runs);
  a `deferred_scope_filter` report claim; a `metadata_explicit` decision memo
  that invents a `## Decision needed` section; `evo_replace_metric`;
  one compaction status.
- **GLM Flash, 14.** Two `task-workflow` samples and three report-claim
  failures; two `pendingProposal` wakes that set a status while a proposal was
  open; two `query-actions` `label` answers claiming a label was already
  assigned when the fixture has none; `gh_complex_latest_on_target`;
  `lateStart`; three compaction statuses.
- **Control, 6.** Two `active_deployment_constraint` decision memos; three goal
  tool-call misses (`evo_replace_metric`, `cx_gym_done_steps_collapse`,
  `gh_complex_habit_behind`); `crowdedDay`.
- **Kimi K3, 26.** As described above.

`evo_replace_metric` is the only exercise missed by three of the four models.

## Harness defects found and fixed

- **Provider refusals failed jobs.** GLM Flash lost 11 jobs to Melious `503`s in
  one run, one of them the `query-reports` fixture, which blocked 18 more. The
  relay now retries an unbilled 429/503/529 (#4338); none of the day's 51 `503`
  responses carried a charge. Gateway 502/504 are deliberately not retried.
- **Network drops failed jobs.** 37 DNS failures, 8 TLS failures and 5 dropped
  connections came in bursts from this machine's network. The relay retries the
  connection before a request is sent (#4336).
- **Identical prompts ran in parallel**, so repeats could not reuse the
  provider's prompt cache (#4339, above).
