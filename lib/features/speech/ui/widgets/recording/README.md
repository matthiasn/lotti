# Recording UI

The visible part of recording: one recorder on every surface — a live level
line over the discard, pause and timed Stop controls — and the recording
strip that carries a take across the app while it runs.

A microphone starts the take at once on the strip at the bottom of the
screen: it says what the take is for, holds pause and Stop with the running
time, opens into the full recorder when tapped, and takes the user back to
where the take started. Stop leaves a short receipt with Undo. Goal and
person check-ins record in place instead. No recorder shows an orb, a meter,
a hint or a model name.
