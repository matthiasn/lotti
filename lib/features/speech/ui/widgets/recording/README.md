# Recording UI

The visible part of recording: one recorder on every surface — a live level
line over the discard, pause and timed Stop controls — and the recording
strip, the task bar and the sidebar card that carry a take across the
app while it runs.

A microphone starts the take at once. On its own task the mic flies down
from the task's action bar and becomes the round Stop at the end of a
recording row: the live dot, the level, the running time and pause. Away
from the task, the take shows with the way back: on a phone, a slim glass
strip along the bottom edge whose body takes the user back; on a wide
window, a card in the sidebar's activity that does the same (the strip
returns while the sidebar is collapsed). Stop leaves a short receipt with
Undo. Goal and person check-ins record in place instead. No recorder shows
an orb, a meter, a hint or a model name.
